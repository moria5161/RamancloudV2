# Research showcase sources

Paper metadata and the 15 unmodified illustrations were taken from the original
RamanCloud V2 research showcase at commit
`249b1bb0894f48413621ae51e08a49d9cc7a3891` (retrieved 2026-11-03).

- Original list: https://github.com/moria5161/RamancloudV2/blob/249b1bb0894f48413621ae51e08a49d9cc7a3891/frontend/src/data/research.js
- Original illustrations: https://github.com/moria5161/RamancloudV2/tree/249b1bb0894f48413621ae51e08a49d9cc7a3891/frontend/public/research
- Upstream repository license is retained in `UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt`.
- `manifest.json` records the original byte sizes and SHA-256 checksums; source
  blobs were checked against the pinned Git tree during retrieval.

The illustrations credit their original authors and publishers. Each card links
to the paper's publisher or DOI page. Chinese titles are the translations used
by the original V2 showcase. This is a maintained selection, not a live feed or
a claim to list every recent publication. Add or update entries in
`frontend/src/content/research.js` and add corresponding local images here.
