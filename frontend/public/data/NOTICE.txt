world-countries.json is derived from Natural Earth 1:50m admin-0 countries.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Natural Earth map data is in the public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Geometry coordinates are rounded to 0.001 degrees for display. LABEL_X/LABEL_Y
are representative map-label locations, not visitor coordinates. Region counts
come from RamanCloud statistics and are not supplied by Natural Earth.
